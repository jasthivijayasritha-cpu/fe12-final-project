How FoodCare Works

🏪

1. Donate

Owners post their surplus food with quantity, location and expiry details.

📍

2. Match

Users find available food based on their location.

🍱

3. Claim

Charities or recipients claim the available food.

📧

4. Notify

Users receive email/SMS notifications about their claims.

🌱

5. Reduce Waste

Surplus food is distributed instead of being wasted.
